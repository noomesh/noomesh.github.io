Add-ons

Extras that run beside the core

Noomesh is a free, self-hosted core. Add-ons are extras that run as their own containers next to it, on the same machines. Some are free and come with Noomesh. Two are licensed, and you request those from us.

Free

Switch these on in noomesh.yaml. No licence needed.

Trellion knowledge pack

Free

Search your own documents and conversation history, build a knowledge graph from what you ingest, and recall earlier work.

addons.trellion

Identity

Free

Short-lived connection tokens for each agent.

addons.identity

Request a licensed add-on

Send us a short note and we will get back to you to arrange your entitlement and answer questions.

Email a request or write to sales@noomesh.ai

The button opens your mail app with a short form to fill in. The add-on terms are shown, and you accept them, when you install your entitlement.

Once you have an entitlement

You receive a token. Installing it prints the terms and asks you to type accept:

noo security entitlement install <token>
noo addons enable trellion-intel
noo stack restart --force

The add-ons run on your own hardware and send nothing back to us.
