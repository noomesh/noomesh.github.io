Self-hosted AI infrastructure

The machines you already own, running as one AI cluster.

Noomesh turns a pile of GPUs, workstations and spare boxes into a single system: model serving, memory, a knowledge graph, and agents that can act — all behind your own network, with nothing leaving it unless you say so.

Runs on your hardware · No data leaves your network by default

What it is

Not a wrapper around someone else's API.

Noomesh is the layer between your hardware and your work: it decides what runs where, keeps what it learns, and gives agents a way to act on it.

Cluster orchestration

Nodes enroll, heartbeat, and report GPU, VRAM and load. Inference backends start on the node that can actually serve them and stop when they go idle.

vLLM · Ollama · llama.cpp

Memory that persists

Conversations, documents and embeddings live in your own Postgres. Context carries across sessions instead of being re-pasted into a prompt each time.

PostgreSQL · pgvector

A knowledge graph

Entities and typed relationships extracted from your documents, queryable alongside vector search — so retrieval can follow connections, not just similarity.

Agents that act

A coordinator runs a real tool-calling loop and streams its reasoning as it works. Multi-agent roles collaborate on longer jobs.

Yours by default

Reachable over your own tailnet, scoped service keys for anything external, and an approval gate in front of actions that touch the real world.

A console and a phone

A web console for the cluster, the graph, jobs and usage — plus a focused mobile companion for the parts you need when you're away from it.

How it works

One gateway in front, your hardware behind.

Everything — console, phone, editor, scripts — talks to one gateway over a contract that's checked in and versioned. Nothing guesses at the API.

Enroll your nodes

Each machine joins with a key and starts reporting what it has: GPU, VRAM, cores, load.

The gateway schedules

It picks a node, starts a backend there, and routes work — retiring backends that go idle.

Work builds memory

Conversations, documents and extracted entities accumulate into a store that later work can draw on.

Agents use all of it

The coordinator reasons over that memory, calls tools, and asks before anything consequential.

Same-origin, on your network

The console is served by the gateway itself over your tailnet. No public ingress to open, no CORS to negotiate, no third-party origin holding a session for you.

  • REST and WebSocket surfaces defined by a checked-in contract
  • Typed client generated from that contract, never hand-written
  • Uncontracted calls fail loudly in development, not silently in production
